CLIENT ADVISORY · FAQ

Studio Policies & Specifications

Guidance on instant Digital Vault access, Drop 01 garment sizing, custom studio engraving, and courier dispatch.

All Digital Vault systems—including client intake flows, liability waivers, mobile aftercare portals, and brand kits—unlock immediately inside your order confirmation drawer and via email dispatch. Templates are formatted for Canva (Free & Pro), Notion, and 300 DPI print-ready PDFs under a single-studio commercial license.
Our 480gsm crewnecks, hoodies, and 300gsm tees are cut in an intentional relaxed studio silhouette across XS–XXL; choose your true size for an architectural drape or size down for a closer fit. Embroidered men's dress shirts (S–XXL) follow tailored studio proportions, and waffle robes are dual-sized (S/M, L/XL). Machine wash cold inside-out with mild detergent and hang or lay flat to preserve embroidery tension.
Open Quick View on the Acrylic QR Block or Branded Entrance Floor Mat to input your exact studio wordmark and optionally attach your vector/PNG QR matrix. Our studio team verifies alignment and contrast before precision laser engraving or woven mat printing; bespoke items dispatch in 3–5 business days.
Digital Vault orders deliver instantly with $0 shipping. Ready-to-ship physical apparel, towels, robes, and desk organizers leave our New York atelier within 48 hours. Studio courier shipping is complimentary on all orders of $120 or more, and $12 flat rate on physical orders under $120.
Unworn, unwashed Drop 01 garments and unopened physical studio objects are eligible for size exchange or refund within 14 days of delivery. Custom-engraved Acrylic QR Blocks, bespoke studio floor mats, and instant-access Digital Vault products are final sale.
